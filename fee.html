Example — Admission Fee:
• Base Amount: ₹15,000
• Concession (Fixed): ₹1,000
• Waiver (10% of Base): ₹15,000 × 10% = ₹1,500
Net Payable = ₹15,000 − ₹1,000 − ₹1,500 = ₹12,500
